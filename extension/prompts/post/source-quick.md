## 6. SOURCE TYPE: 随便聊聊


The user selected "随便聊聊".

There is no external source.

The user simply wants some posts based on their own idea, thought, or general inspiration.

The user's idea belongs to one of three directions:

### 顶级认知

A counter-intuitive but defensible judgement.

The post should contain an actual insight or observation.

It should make the reader think "好像确实如此", rather than simply sounding profound.

Prefer concrete observations over abstract philosophical statements.

### 冷知识

A specific, interesting, and checkable fact.

The fact must be something you are confident is true and can reasonably be verified.

Never guess.

Never use a fact that you only vaguely remember.

The fact should ideally relate naturally to the user's idea when an idea is provided.

### 扎心真相

An uncomfortable but recognizable truth.

State it plainly.

Do not preach.

Do not moralize.

Do not turn it into motivational advice.

Determine which direction the user's idea belongs to.

All candidates stay in that direction — the writing style changes how the post is expressed, not what it is about.

Do not mix directions across candidates — every candidate opens with an opening line from the same direction.

If the user's idea does not clearly fit any of the three directions, pick the one that fits it best.

### Category opening line

Every candidate must begin with a short category opening line as its own first paragraph.

The line names the category in the natural, conversational way real X users write it.

Wording is free — invent fresh variants in the same spirit. Examples per category:

顶级认知 — 分享一个顶级认知 / 一个顶级认知：

冷知识 — 一个冷知识： / 一个99%的人都不知道的冷知识： / 知道这个冷知识，你就超越了99%的人：

扎心真相 — 一个扎心真相： / 一个99%的人都不知道的扎心真相： / 说个很扎心的事实：

These are examples, not templates.

Vary the opener wording across the candidates — do not make them mechanical copies of one pattern.

Slightly exaggerated or teasing openers are part of the native X flavor — do not sanitize them into
something flat.

The opening line is part of the format for this source type, not an empty introduction.

It must be the first paragraph, followed by a blank line before the body.

This applies to every candidate, regardless of the writing style used for it.

Study the shape and the voice — the opening line, the clipped chunks, the emotion.

These are examples of format and voice only — not the output structure. Each comes from a different direction; the output must stay in the single direction of the user's idea.

Never copy their topics, wording, or sentences into generated posts.
